## Reference vs. prospect vs. connector

- **Reference:** someone whose public identity or editorial work teaches ATU something. No commercial inference.
- **Prospect hypothesis:** a recognizable *situation* that may benefit from ATU; investigate the need before adding a person to an actual outreach plan.
- **Connector:** a person or organization whose work may lead to introductions, co-created editorial content, or insight into the market.

The same person could fall into more than one category, but don't assume it.

## How to add a future person

Use a heading with name and role, then:
- Direct Instagram / website / long interview links.
- **Public evidence:** what is actually observable, with source and date.
- **What to study:** one focused question.
- **ATU hypothesis:** potential relevance, not a diagnosis or presumed need.
- **Status:** reference / prospect archetype / connector / verified conversation (only with real evidence).
- **Next action:** save a concrete example, watch a talk, or have a voluntary conversation.

**No scraping private details, no assumptions about someone's finances, psychology, dissatisfaction or willingness to buy.**
