# Competitive landscape and adjacent practices

*ATU research desk · 2026-11-19 · initial market scan*

The aim is not to find an enemy or invent an empty market. It is to understand which disciplines people already pay for, what credible providers actually offer, and where **personal insight + creative direction + business judgment** might form a useful relationship.

Start with [the positioning map](../strategy/positioning-map.svg) and [positioning hypotheses](../strategy/positioning.md). This research is a snapshot; pricing, team sizes, client rosters and current availability require checking directly.

## Competitor category map

| Category | What a buyer may expect | ATU's potential space | Main challenge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal branding studio | Strategy, photography, visual identity, content, roll-out | A relationship that starts with a person's life direction and strategic choices | Must be concrete enough to justify a fee |
| Coach / leadership practitioner | Development, reflection, decisions, behavior | Integrate judgment about brand, business, style, and execution | Must maintain ethical boundaries and avoid therapy claims |
| Creative director / image specialist | Visual taste and style choices | Tie expression to authentic values and commercial opportunities | Need proof beyond mood boards |
| Luxury strategic consultant | Premium audiences, positioning, experience, access | Human-first perspective independent of a particular company | Trusted access may be hard to earn |
| Content / PR agency | Publishing cadence, reach, relationships and media | A worldview worth publishing before campaign mechanics | No assumption that content alone causes strategic value |

## ATU positioning hypothesis

**A discreet and discerning outside perspective that joins three fields:**

- *See clearly*: grasp who this person is, what matters and where they're headed.
- *See differently*: explore previously overlooked strategic and creative possibilities.
- *Make it real*: shape expression, relationships, projects and next moves through appropriate collaborators and tools.

The attractive part is the **combination**, not a new title like “personal-brand coach.” But the market could also find this combination confusing, expensive or unnecessary. We need customer conversations to test it.

### What we should not promise prematurely

- That clients will become wealthy, famous or get more followers.
- That personality discovery alone produces business results.
- That we will provide licensed psychological or therapeutic services.
- That we produce all photography, content or branding in-house.
- That named people need ATU or are interested in working together.

## How to use this research

For each provider, save:
1. Screenshot or URL of the actual homepage opening.
2. Exact wording of the current proposition, briefly paraphrased with source.
3. Services and engagement model **only when publicly stated**.
4. Three content examples, with direct URLs.
5. Evidence of proof (case studies, testimonials, attributable results).
6. What is missing or difficult to understand **from the outside**.
7. The one distinction ATU would need to demonstrate, not merely claim.

## Three commercial hypotheses to test

- **The next-chapter client:** accomplished, with resources and options, looking for an intelligent sounding board for what's next.
- **The founder whose presence trails the business:** strong business, little distinct personal point of view publicly expressed. Needs assessment, not automatic branding.
- **The public person evolving into a new field:** a coherent voice, strategy and collaborators are useful as they move toward a new venture.

Do not presume any particular public figure fits these circumstances privately.
