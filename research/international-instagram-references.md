# International Instagram references — editorial identities with repeatable formats

*ATU research · 2026-11-19 · curated for the ATU / Figma / Parabola social mood board*

**Goal:** find people with strong, recognizable voices and feeds that derive their impact from language, presence, images, and consistent constraints—not primarily expensive production. This is **not a ranking of reach**, nor proof that they have small teams: staffing and current production budgets were not verified. Several have grown into established businesses; study the early, reproducible format rather than assuming the whole operation is cheap.

Instagram itself was not consistently accessible for frame-by-frame inspection. These descriptions are grounded in official sites, creator interviews, selected visual work, and external coverage. Check current feeds and save original post URLs/screenshots before final design decisions.

## Closest visual/editorial references

### Sarah Bahbah — images as scenes, captions as subtext

- [Instagram @sarahbahbah](https://www.instagram.com/sarahbahbah/)
- [Artist's series](https://www.sarahbahbah.com/i-love-you-me-neither)
- [LensCulture visual interview](https://www.lensculture.com/articles/sarah-bahbah-photography-as-an-expression-of-indulgence)
- [Dazed on her cinematic stills](https://editors.dazeddigital.com/art-photography/article/38649/1/sarah-bahbahs-coming-of-age-art-for-the-instagram-generation)
- **Format:** still photograph with a short line at the bottom, like a subtitle in a film. Emotion comes partly from the gap between what we see and what the line says.
- **For ATU:** translate her visual grammar to ordinary strong portraits, not necessarily stylized productions. Use a small statement that changes the meaning of the expression in the photo.
- **Caveat:** her full productions can be far more elaborate than a solo social workflow. Borrow the editorial device, not the cost.

### Allison Bornstein — approachable expert with a memorable method

- [Instagram @allisonbornstein6](https://www.instagram.com/allisonbornstein6/)
- [Creator's content archive](https://www.allisonbornstein.com/content)
- [Creator's about page](https://www.allisonbornstein.com/about)
- [Vogue on the three-word method](https://www.vogue.com/article/allison-bornstein-wear-it-well)
- **Format:** simple explanatory videos, portraits, style demonstrations, practical naming devices (“three words”, “wrong shoe”); accessible rather than overproduced.
- **For ATU:** develop a *lens* that makes complicated decisions easy to discuss, perhaps without turning that lens into a branded coaching framework. Use understated portrait + one short insight. One idea per Reel.

### Leandra Medine Cohen — unmistakable authorial voice

- [Instagram @leandramcohen](https://www.instagram.com/leandramcohen/)
- [The Cereal Aisle](https://leandramcohen.substack.com/)
- [Newsletter description](https://leandramcohen.substack.com/about)
- **Format:** lived-in personal style, observation, wit, images of daily life, strong captions/essays; not primarily a fixed on-image-text account.
- **For ATU:** an interesting *personality* should carry over between highly designed tiles and unpolished life. Taste and humor can coexist. Avoid a dead-serious “premium coach” voice.

### Jules Acree — low-pressure intentional living

- [Instagram @julesacree](https://www.instagram.com/julesacree/)
- [Official content library](https://www.julesacree.com/explore)
- [About / editorial direction](https://www.julesacree.com/about)
- **Format:** home and working-life visuals, thoughtful first-person voice, reflective video, ordinary scenes treated warmly; usable principles without hype.
- **For ATU:** a photograph of everyday life + a short, honest question can be enough. Alternate portrait, still-life, informal speaking, and unbranded journal entries.

## Low-lift publishing grammars

### Austin Kleon — the feed as a working notebook

- [Instagram @austinkleon](https://www.instagram.com/austinkleon/)
- [Four notebooks](https://austinkleon.com/2025/01/08/my-4-notebooks/)
- [Creative tools / production](https://austinkleon.com/gear/)
- **Format:** photographed journal pages, clippings, annotations, quotations, small work in progress.
- **For ATU:** show *how you observe and think*, not only the polished finished statement. One notebook photo can be a whole post. Strong human texture without a shoot.

### Adam J. Kurtz — irreverent, human, text-first

- [Instagram @adamjk](https://www.instagram.com/adamjk/)
- [Artist's description](https://adamjk.com/pages/about)
- [Interview specifically about his Instagram approach](https://sixtysixmag.com/adam-j-kurtz/)
- **Format:** large handwritten thoughts, bold simple fields, humor and vulnerability without slickness.
- **For ATU:** test typographic personality that can occasionally break the elegant system, showing character. Do not turn every serious idea into a motivational quote.

### Jack Butcher / Visualize Value — an idea becomes a graphic

- [Instagram @visualizevalue](https://www.instagram.com/visualizevalue/)
- [Founder's own project archive](https://www.jack.art/visualize-value)
- [Visual gallery](https://visualizevalue.com/visuals)
- [Founder's account of growing the practice through posting](https://visualizevalue.com/answers/how-to-build-an-audience-from-zero)
- **Format:** black canvas, white text, minimal line geometry; one concept per image. Became recognizable precisely because of severe constraints.
- **For ATU:** a third recurring format could be idea-diagrams, away from portrait photography. Draw a relationship between choice / style / consequence using almost nothing.

### Yung Pueblo — ultra-simple textual reflections

- [Instagram @yung_pueblo](https://www.instagram.com/yung_pueblo/)
- [Analysis of minimalist visual style](https://www.unity.org/en/article/listening-yung-pueblo)
- **Format:** lowercase sentences, plain white ground, unembellished typography.
- **For ATU:** a very cheap, instantly recognizable template with high readability and a repeatable visual rule.
- **Caveat:** risk of generic self-help and ego stroking. ATU needs sharper observations and fewer universal “truths.”

### Morgan Harper Nichols — visual poems and audience participation

- [Instagram @morganharpernichols](https://www.instagram.com/morganharpernichols/)
- [Artist biography](https://morganharpernichols.com/bio)
- [Analysis of text/art interplay](https://www.diggitmagazine.com/articles/influence-instagram-s-affordances-instapoetry-characteristics-literary-genre-born-social)
- **Format:** warm artwork/collage with written lines; stories and responses from the audience influence the work.
- **For ATU:** content can emerge from the questions and reflections of the people around the practice, with permission, rather than being invented by a content team.

## Camera-first voice and editorial intelligence

### Therapy Jeff / Jeff Guenther — the Reel is the personality

- [Instagram @therapyjeff](https://www.instagram.com/therapyjeff/)
- [Own about page](https://www.therapyjeff.com/about)
- [2026 profile of his content and visual language](https://www.wweek.com/culture/2026/01/14/therapy-jeff-is-not-your-therapist/)
- **Format:** direct-to-camera one-minute videos; particular cadence and humor; recognizable Reel covers and direct captions.
- **For ATU:** a phone, a face, an observation, and a clear rhythm. Authenticity doesn't require a professionally directed shoot. Don't adopt clinical claims or therapy advice.

### Rachel Karten — the researcher's eye and a personal feed

- [Instagram @milkkarten](https://www.instagram.com/milkkarten/)
- [Link in Bio](https://www.linkinbio.news/)
- [Personal consultancy](https://milkkarten.com/)
- [Conversation about her Instagram mix](https://embedded.substack.com/p/link-in-bio-rachel-karten-social-media-tips)
- [Essay on photography instead of always chasing video](https://www.linkinbio.news/p/photos-still-matter-on-brand-social)
- **Format:** professional commentary alongside personal photos, food, dogs, odd findings, observations. A useful example of an expert not pretending every post is a sales asset.
- **For ATU:** keep the private individual's curiosity visible. Observe, collect, explain, then occasionally promote.

## ATU mood-board exercise

Start with **6 repeatable structures**, using the same fictional persona and supplied images:

| Variant | Example treatment | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portrait + one quiet line | Image ~90%, type anchored bottom | Emotional resonance; Sarah Bahbah principle |
| Phone Reel + small subtitles | Face close, natural cadence, one idea | Credible voice; Therapy Jeff / Allison Bornstein principle |
| Solid navy + one sentence | No photo; generous margins | A calm visual signature; Yung Pueblo as extreme baseline |
| One idea drawn with two shapes | Minimal geometry + 2–5 words | Intellectual authority; Visualize Value principle |
| Candid photo + short diary note | Unstyled personal material, small copy | Character; Leandra / Jules Acree principle |
| Notebook / found detail / annotation | Physical material, handwriting or simple type | Observational practice; Austin Kleon principle |

**Do not make every tile a quote card.** Alternate perspectives, people, places, work, questions, humor and texture.

### Deconstruction checklist

For **three posts per reference** (save direct post URLs later when Instagram is inspectable), note:
- What is the image? Portrait, phone still, work object, found photo, or flat field?
- How much text and where? 2 words, sentence, subtitle, paragraph?
- What is the voice? Assertive, tentative, funny, observant, confessional?
- What is actually *interesting*? A distinct idea, a story, a contrast, a useful method?
- What is the signature repeatable rule? Type, placement, mood, rhythm, format?
- What would it take to reproduce the *format* with one person and a phone?
- What is unique to the creator and should not be copied?

Keep original URLs and proof separate from your own visual/system interpretations.
