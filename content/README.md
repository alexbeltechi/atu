# ATU content library

*An editable bank of original prompts, editorial fragments and reusable visual formats, assembled 2026-11-19.*

This is **raw material**, not a content calendar or approved published copy. It came from explorations of a fictional founder/creator persona used to stress-test an adaptable Figma / Parabola brand mood board. The same formats should work for a man, woman, artist, advisor, founder or brand. Not every piece needs to sound like ATU or sell something.

## Useful editorial mix

Use as an experiment, **not a rule**:
- About a third: observations, questions and ideas.
- About a third: people, portraits and conversations.
- About a third: moments, culture, projects and everyday life.

Balance thoughtful and playful. A human feed needs casual details as well as strong opinions. Sometimes let a photograph carry the post with no text at all.

## When generating more

1. Pick a **persona** with actual interests, contradictions and goals. Do not equate polished imagery with depth.
2. Pick a **content role**: observation / question / opinion / story / education / diary / behind-the-scenes / work / invitation.
3. Pick a **medium**: still portrait, story, three-slide carousel, Reel, typographic frame, photo diary, interview clip.
4. Adapt the idea to their own language and experience; never fabricate a personal confession, event or testimonial.
5. Write for a person speaking naturally, not a motivational quote generator.
6. Record effective new ideas in the appropriate heading of [idea-bank.md](idea-bank.md) or [copy-bank.md](copy-bank.md).

## Brand-system test

A good template lets you swap:
`persona`, `message`, `headline`, `body`, `image`, `font`, `colors`, `logo`, `content type`, `alignment`, and `text-background style` without rebuilding the post. Use the same content across several treatments; the system should adapt to the content.
