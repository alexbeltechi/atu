# Atú

An evolving Romanian editorial and advisory project about **personal depth, judgment, expression, and what people choose to do next**.

Atú explores the space between understanding a person, seeing possibilities around them, and bringing their direction into the world. The point is not to manufacture a persona, chase follower counts, or rush to a packaged service.

**Stage:** research and editorial exploration · **Updated:** 2026-11-19 · **Positioning:** hypothesis, not approved.

## What we're building now

A distinctive body of thought and visual work, published primarily on Instagram, with an intentionally minimal `atu.studio` business-card site: name, a line worth remembering, and a way to get in touch. The site is not a traditional sales landing page.

**Work on the ideas before finalizing the offer.** The potential commercial relationship is selective, personal and advisory; its precise shape, pricing and duration have not been decided.

## Working principles

- Start with the person's values, motivations, aspirations and decisions; visual identity is an expression, not the starting point.
- Say something worthwhile. Avoid self-congratulatory, motivational or luxury-for-its-own-sake copy.
- A public presence demonstrates judgment; a private conversation determines whether and how to work together.
- Investigate real client needs rather than assuming that successful people require personal branding.
- AI can help build and explore, but isn't the public proposition.
- Preserve attribution: **observed public fact**, **ATU interpretation**, and **possible client hypothesis** are different things.
- Never imply that a named public figure needs ATU or is an actual lead without their involvement.
- No confidential client data, private Brain content, contact information or sensitive personal notes in this public repository.
